How late will my bus be today?

An hour-by-hour forecast of how late each Metro Transit route will run today. A model trained on the history of Metro Transit's own live feed makes the forecast each morning, and every forecast is scored against what actually happened.

How it works

  • Data. Every 15 minutes, a GitHub Action reads Metro Transit's GTFS-Realtime trip updates and records how far behind schedule every active bus and train is at its next stop.
  • Target. Average lateness per route per hour.
  • Features. The model uses only what's known by 4 am:
    • route, hour, day of week and holidays;
    • that route-hour's lateness yesterday, a week ago, and averaged over the last 14 days;
    • the route's average yesterday;
    • hourly weather (temperature, rain, snow, wind) from Open-Meteo.
  • Model. A LightGBM regressor with absolute-error loss. It is compared against two baselines: same hour last week, and the trailing 14-day average.
